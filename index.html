Daily Islamic Posts

A free, non-commercial project that shares one ayah of the Quran and one authentic hadith from Sahih al-Bukhari every day, in Urdu and English, as a sadaqah jariah.

What it posts

Each day: 4 images (ayah and hadith, in Urdu and English) and 2 short videos with recitation. The Quran goes in order from the first ayah to the last, then starts again. Hadith come in order from Sahih al-Bukhari.

How it works

  1. Each morning the app takes the next ayah and hadith with their translations from trusted public sources.
  2. It designs the images and short videos.
  3. It publishes them to our own accounts only (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, TikTok and Pinterest) using each platform's official API.
  4. It reads the public numbers of our own posts (views, likes, followers) so we can see what helps people most.

The app does not post for other people, does not log in to anyone else's account, and does not collect anyone's personal data.
